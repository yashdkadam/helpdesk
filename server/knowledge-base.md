# Customer Knowledge Base

This knowledge base is used by the AI auto-resolve system to answer common customer questions about our online IT course platform.

---

## Account & Password

### How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click **"Forgot password?"**. Enter the email address associated with your account and we will send you a password reset link. The link expires after 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email, check your spam/junk folder. If the issue persists, contact support.

### How do I change my email address?
Log in to your account, go to **Account Settings → Profile**, and update your email address. You will receive a confirmation email to the new address. You must click the confirmation link before the change takes effect.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to **Account Settings → Danger Zone** and click **"Delete Account"**. This is permanent and cannot be undone. All your course progress, certificates, and purchase history will be lost. If you want to take a break instead, you can simply stop logging in — your data is retained indefinitely.

### I can't log in even though my password is correct.
Try the following steps:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies and try again.
2. Make sure Caps Lock is off.
3. Try a different browser or an incognito/private window.
4. If you signed up via Google or GitHub, use the corresponding "Sign in with Google/GitHub" button instead of email/password.
5. If none of the above work, use the **"Forgot password?"** flow to set a new password.

## Courses & Enrollment

### How do I enroll in a course?
Browse the course catalog, open the course page, and click **"Enroll Now"**. Free courses are accessible immediately. Paid courses require you to complete checkout first. Once enrolled, the course appears in your **My Courses** dashboard.

### Can I access a course after I finish it?
Yes. Once purchased, you have lifetime access to the course content. You can revisit any lecture, re-download resources, and retake quizzes as many times as you like.

### How do I continue where I left off?
Go to **My Courses**, click the course, and the platform will automatically take you to your last watched lecture. Your progress is saved automatically every time you complete a video.

### Are courses available in languages other than English?
Most courses are recorded in English. Some courses include subtitles in Hindi, Spanish, or Portuguese. Check the course page under the **"Languages"** section to see available subtitle options.

### Can I download course videos for offline viewing?
Video downloads are available on the mobile app for enrolled students. Downloads are not available via the web browser due to content protection. Downloaded videos expire after 30 days and must be renewed by going online.

## Billing & Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and UPI (for Indian customers). We do not currently accept bank transfers or cryptocurrency.

### Can I get a receipt or invoice for my purchase?
Yes. Go to **Account Settings → Purchase History**, find the transaction, and click **"Download Invoice"**. The invoice includes your name, purchase date, course name, amount, and tax details.

### Is there a free trial?
We offer a free preview of the first few lectures for most courses. You can access these without creating an account. Full access requires enrollment. Some courses are completely free — these are marked with a **"Free"** badge on the catalog page.

### Do you offer discounts for students or teams?
Yes. Students with a valid `.edu` email address can apply for a 30% discount via **Account Settings → Student Discount**. For team or corporate enrollments (5+ seats), contact sales at sales@example.com for a custom quote.

## Refunds

### What is your refund policy?
We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on all paid courses. If you are not satisfied for any reason, you can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase — no questions asked. Refunds are not available after 30 days or if you have completed more than 80% of the course.

### How do I request a refund?
Submit a support ticket with your order number and the reason for the refund. Our team will process it within 2–3 business days. The refund will be credited back to your original payment method. Processing time depends on your bank (typically 5–10 business days after we issue it).

### I was charged twice for the same course. What should I do?
This can happen if you clicked "Pay" multiple times. Contact support immediately with your order numbers and we will refund the duplicate charge within 24 hours.

## Certificates

### Do I get a certificate after completing a course?
Yes. Upon completing all lectures and passing the final quiz (if applicable) with a score of 70% or higher, a certificate of completion is automatically generated and available in **My Certificates**.

### How do I share my certificate on LinkedIn?
Open the certificate from **My Certificates**, click **"Add to LinkedIn"**, and it will pre-fill the credential details. Alternatively, download the PDF and upload it manually to your LinkedIn profile under **Licenses & Certifications**.

### My certificate shows the wrong name. How do I fix it?
Your certificate uses the name on your account profile. Go to **Account Settings → Profile**, update your full name, then go to **My Certificates** and click **"Regenerate Certificate"**.

### Is the certificate accredited or recognized by employers?
Our certificates are industry-recognized but not formally accredited by a government body. They demonstrate practical skill completion and are widely accepted by IT employers. For formal accreditation, look for courses marked with a **CompTIA / AWS / Microsoft** partner badge on the course page.

## Technical Issues

### Videos are buffering or not playing.
Try the following:
1. Switch to a lower video quality (360p or 480p) using the quality selector in the video player.
2. Pause the video for 30 seconds to let it buffer.
3. Check your internet connection — we recommend at least 5 Mbps for HD playback.
4. Disable browser extensions (especially ad blockers) and try again.
5. Try a different browser — we recommend Chrome or Firefox for best compatibility.
6. Clear your browser cache and reload the page.

### The course platform is slow or not loading.
Check our status page at status.example.com for any ongoing incidents. If there are no known issues, try clearing your cache, disabling VPN, or using a different network.

### I can't access a course I already purchased.
Log out and log back in to refresh your session. If the course still does not appear, go to **My Courses → Restore Purchases**. If the issue persists, contact support with your order number and we will restore access manually within a few hours.

### Quiz answers are not saving.
This is usually caused by a session timeout or a lost internet connection during submission. Make sure you have a stable connection, then refresh the page and reattempt the quiz. Quiz attempts are tracked — if you already submitted, your score will appear in the results tab.

### The course code/lab environment isn't working.
Our lab environments require WebSockets. Make sure your browser allows WebSocket connections and that your firewall or corporate proxy does not block them. Try on a personal network if you're on a corporate or university network. If the problem persists, include your browser version and operating system in a support ticket.

## Subscriptions & Plans

### What is the difference between individual and pro plans?
The **Individual Plan** gives access to the standard course catalog. The **Pro Plan** includes everything in Individual plus: live Q&A sessions with instructors, early access to new courses, downloadable project files, and priority support with a 4-hour response time SLA.

### How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Go to **Account Settings → Subscription** and select your desired plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to **Account Settings → Subscription → Cancel Subscription**. You will retain access until the end of your paid period. We do not offer partial refunds for unused subscription time.

### My subscription renewed but I didn't want it to.
Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the renewal date. We send a reminder email 7 days before renewal. If you missed the cancellation window, contact support within 48 hours of the charge and we will evaluate a refund on a case-by-case basis.

## Contact & Support

### How do I contact support?
Submit a ticket through this helpdesk portal. Our response times are:
- **Free plan**: within 48 business hours
- **Individual plan**: within 24 business hours
- **Pro plan**: within 4 business hours

### What information should I include in a support ticket?
To help us resolve your issue faster, please include:
- Your registered email address
- Order number (for billing issues)
- Course name (for course-related issues)
- Browser and operating system
- A screenshot or screen recording if applicable

### Do you offer phone support?
We do not currently offer phone support. All support is handled through tickets and, for Pro plan members, scheduled video calls with an instructor for course-related questions.
